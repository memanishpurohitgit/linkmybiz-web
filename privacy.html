1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information LinkMyBiz ("we", "us") collects when you use the LinkMyBiz mobile app, and how we use, share, and protect it. By using LinkMyBiz, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: your email address and password (used only for sign-in; passwords are never stored in plain text). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.

Profile information: full name, age, state, city, address, PIN code, phone number, email, business/individual type, and an optional profile or company logo photo.

Content you post: business opportunity listings, "looking for business" listings, startup fundraising profiles (including financial details, founder information, and pitch materials you choose to upload), investor profiles, export/import listings, and any applications you submit -- including optional file attachments (PDF, Word documents, images, or video).

Contact preferences: your Allow Calls / Allow Emails settings, which control whether other users can see your phone number or email.

Usage information: when you last opened the app (used to distinguish active from inactive accounts), and a record of when you tap Call or Email on another user's listing (used only for internal usage reporting -- see Section 5).

Support messages: anything you submit through the in-app "Reach Us" feedback form.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and operate your account, and show your postings to other users.
  • To let other users find and contact you when you post an opportunity, listing, or application -- subject to your Allow Calls / Allow Emails settings.
  • To send you transactional emails: sign-up verification codes, password reset codes, and notifications about your applications.
  • To respond to support requests submitted via "Reach Us".
  • To understand overall app usage (how many postings, applications, and active users we have) so we can maintain and improve the app.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Information Sharing

Your phone number and email are never shown to other users unless you turn on the corresponding Allow Calls / Allow Emails toggle yourself, or you take an action that shares it as part of normal app use (for example, being selected for an interview on an application you submitted).

We share data with the following service providers, solely to operate the app:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseDatabase, authentication, and file storage for the app
ResendDelivery of transactional emails (verification codes, password resets, notifications)
GoogleOptional "Continue with Google" sign-in

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Internal Usage Analytics

A small number of internal-only reports (postings per category, active user counts, application status counts, and call/email tap counts) are visible to the app's operator for the purpose of understanding and improving the app. These reports are aggregate/summary in nature and are not shared outside LinkMyBiz.

6. Data Security

Data is stored with Supabase over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to your data is restricted by row-level security rules so that, other than what you choose to make visible through the app's normal features, only you can read or edit your own profile and postings.

7. Your Privacy Rights

  • You can view and edit your profile information at any time from within the app.
  • You can turn Allow Calls / Allow Emails on or off at any time from Settings.
  • You can deactivate your account from Settings, which signs you out and clears your name, contact details, and photo from view to other users.
  • You can request full deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us at the address below.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you deactivate your account, your personal details are scrubbed from view but postings or messages already shared with other users prior to deactivation may remain. If you request full deletion, we will remove your account and associated personal data, subject to any legal retention requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

LinkMyBiz is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Resend) may process and store data on servers located outside India. We take reasonable steps to ensure your information is handled securely wherever it is processed.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the app evolves. Continuing to use LinkMyBiz after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to memanishpurohit@gmail.com, or via the "Reach Us" form inside the app's Settings.
